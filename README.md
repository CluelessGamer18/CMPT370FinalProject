This is our Final Project for CMPT370 at Macewan University
Welcome to Platforms!
Your goal is to reach the top of the map while collecting as
many coins as you can without dying!

Using WASD to move and Spacebar to jump you can move around the screen.
You can also use the mouse to make your camera rotate around the Y axis. 
Pressing "m" changes your camera mode to a freecam that allows you to freely
traverse the playing field with no issue.
Avoid standing in our enemies FOV for too long or it will kill you and send 
you back to the start.

To download you may need to sort out a few of the files from the download as 
there were some pushing problems, other than that feel free to enjoy a very small
and simple game created by two future game devs!
